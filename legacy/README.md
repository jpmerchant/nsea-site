# legacy/

HTML that already exists and is waiting to be wired into the new site. Nothing here is served by the site.

- `tools/boards/`: the 12 board pages from `jpmerchant/nsea-swim-boards`. HTML only. The `data/*.json` files stay in that repo, and the new site embeds the live GitHub Pages URLs until you decide to absorb them.
- `tools/events/`: the events calendar templates (`site-src/`) and builder from `jpmerchant/nsea-events`.
- `tools/standards/`: Check My Time, SCY comparisons, NC championship embeds, board additions.
- `content/`: newsletters, meet recaps, guides.

Files marked `Project:` in MIGRATION.md live in the claude.ai "NSEA Swim" project, not on disk (export them from the project when you get to those pages).
Per-athlete biomechanics reports are intentionally absent.
