Standards combine USA Swimming Motivational (B–AAAA), NC Senior and NC Age Group Championship cuts, ESSZ Sectional cuts, and national meet cuts (Futures, Winter Juniors, Junior Nationals, US Open, Nationals). A national or sectional cut listed under a younger age group is the cut for that age range (e.g. 18&U). "Bonus" cuts are bonus-entry standards. Meet entry rules, qualifying periods and age-up dates apply; always confirm against the meet's published qualifying information. Times are short course yards.
