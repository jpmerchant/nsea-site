NSEA Swim

Team Group Flow

The general progression through NSEA's training groups, based on the typical purpose of each level. This is a guide, not a strict rulebook — it does not capture every component of a group, and shouldn't be read as the only path or requirement for a given swimmer.
1
Splash
AGE 5–9
Intro to Competitive Swimming
Building basic skills and practice habits
2
Tidepool
AGE 8–11
Previous Swim Experience Necessary
Building fundamentals of competitive swimming and racing
3
Riptide
AGE 10–13
Previous Competitive Experience Required
Refining and building long-term habits
4
Breakers
AGE 13 & OLDER
Previous Competitive Training Experience Required
Continuing to build habits while establishing a training and racing base
5
Surge
AGE 14 & OLDER
At Least 1 Year in Breakers (or Similar)
Refining racing habits and skills; expanding training intensity and volume
6
Senior
AGE 15 & OLDER
At Least 1 Year in Surge (or Similar)
Commitment to NSEA as primary activity — training and racing at a regional and national level
