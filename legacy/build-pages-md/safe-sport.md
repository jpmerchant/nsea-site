NSEA Swim is required to implement the new Minor Athlete Abuse Prevention Policy ([MAAPP](joplin-content://note-viewer//tmp/.mount_JoplinB2iCW2/resources/app.asar/gui/note-viewer/index.html#)) by June 23, 2019. All Athletes, Adult Athletes and Non-Athlete members are required to comply by this policy. 

**NSEA Swim's Safe Sport Coordinator is Brittany Witmer**. If you have questions about the [MAAPP](joplin-content://note-viewer//tmp/.mount_JoplinB2iCW2/resources/app.asar/gui/note-viewer/index.html#) or Safe Sport policies, contact Brittany at <brittany@nseaswim.com>.

Additionally, USA Swimming is committed to safeguarding all of its members with the welfare of its athlete members as the top priority. Safe Sport is USA Swimming’s comprehensive abuse prevention program and provides tools for swim clubs to educate their members and intervene in risky situations. Please see the below links as important training tools for your athletes and families.
