Northside Elite Aquatics, or NSEA Swim, is a competitive swim team offering high quality professional coaching and technique instruction for all ages and abilities. The goal of our team is to provide each member an opportunity to improve their swimming and achieve success at his or her level of ability, from novice to international competitor. NSEA Swim is a nonprofit entity and is run by its elected Board of Directors. All members are welcome at each meeting and encouraged to be involved in team activities and fundraisers.

NSEA Swim is a USA Swimming / North Carolina Swimming affiliated club that strives to develop a competitive swim team at both the state and national levels. Our goal is to increase multicultural, ethnic, and socioeconomic diversity in the sport of swimming by fostering a safe and supportive environment at all levels of the sport. In doing so, we want to ensure athletes are given the opportunity to realize their potential and capture their dreams. We believe in fundamentals, love of the sport, and quality coaching as a catalyst for success and realization of those dreams. Through commitment and dedication, NSEA Swim strives to develop athletes who understand the benefits of self-discipline, time management, and goal setting both inside and outside of the pool.
