Six floor exercises to do on deck before you get in the water. They wake up the shoulder blades and upper back for the pull and breathing, and open the hips for kicks, walls, and breaststroke. Follow the order below so you move from all fours to kneeling to lying down without wasted time, and tap each set as you finish it.

**Before you start**

- Use a mat or folded towel under your knees. Pool decks are hard and wet.
- Move **slowly and with control** . These are mobility drills, not a race. You should feel a stretch or muscles working, never pinching or sharp pain.
- Keep your lower back **flat** in every exercise. If it arches or sags, make the movement smaller.
- This is a warm-up for healthy swimmers. If something hurts during swimming, talk to your coach and an athletic trainer or physical therapist.

## How much

One set of each exercise before practice.

The full program: 2 sets of 10. Use it on days with extra time or at home.
