Reading this chart: TritonWear rows are sized to line up with their closest Rushton zone(s) — Zone 4 spans ORANGE+BLUE, Zone 5 spans PURPLE+GREEN+GOLD. This is a rough, RPE-based alignment, not a 1:1 metric equivalence — Rushton is built on stroke rate/tempo/lactate, TritonWear on %HRmax/×CSS/Energy Score. The ×CSS ranges are transcribed exactly as shown on TritonWear's own infographic — Zones 3 & 4 overlap/invert rather than forming a clean ladder, so don't use ×CSS alone for a threshold calculation.
