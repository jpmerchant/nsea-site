Where the numbers come from

All of it comes from the race-analysis files in NSEA's Race Data folder. Every level is a median of real swims with the splits, breakouts and stroke rates the analysts measured. The planner blends the two levels either side of your goal time. Faster than every level uses the fastest; slower than every level uses the slowest.

How a plan is calculated
  • Splits: each level gives the share of the race each length takes. Your goal is split by the blended shares.
  • Off the wall: the median time from the start, or from the wall, to breakout. Swim time is the rest of the length up to the hand touch. On flip turns the touchpad records the feet, so 0.30 s comes off for the hand.
  • Stroke counts: the blended stroke rate × swim time. Free and back count both arms (2 strokes a cycle); breast and fly count 1 a cycle. The grid shows every count whose rate falls in the AG Camp range around that centre.
  • Course conversion: LCM times ÷ 1.11 for SCY (400 m free → 500 y ×1.115, 800 → 1000 ×1.12, 1500 → 1650 ×0.975). It only places a level on the bar; the pacing shape doesn't depend on it.
  • Data checks: swims whose recorded splits are broken (e.g. a 25 of 8.27 then 19.54) are dropped. Events marked modeled have no direct race data and are built from the nearest event, with a note saying how.
