Standards combine USA Swimming Motivational (B–AAAA), NC Senior and NC Age Group Championship cuts, ESSZ Sectional cuts, national meet cuts (Futures, Road to Indy, Winter Juniors, Junior Nationals, US Open, Nationals) and the pool qualifying times for Open Water Nationals; in long course, Olympic Trials too. A national or sectional cut listed under a younger age group is the cut for that age range (e.g. 18&U). "Bonus" cuts are bonus-entry standards. Meet entry rules, qualifying periods and age-up dates apply; always confirm against the meet's published qualifying information. Times are short course yards.
