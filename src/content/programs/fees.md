Practice fees are monthly and depend on your swimmer's group and the season.

| Group | Monthly SCY<br>(Sept – March) | Monthly Spring<br>(April – May) | Monthly Summer<br>(June – July) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Splash | $130 | $100 | $100 |
| Tidepool | $150 | $115 | $115 |
| Riptide | $160 | $120 | $120 |
| Breakers | $170 | $125 | $125 |
| Surge | $185 | $140 | $140 |
| Senior | $220 | $165 | $165 |

New swimmers also need to register with USA Swimming and are responsible for that registration fee.

[Download the 2026–27 fee schedule (PDF)](/assets/2026-27-practice-fee-schedule.pdf)

## Seasons

We run three seasons. Spring and Summer are opt-out, so you don't need to do anything to continue. See [Session & Season Opt-Out](/families/how-to/session-opt-out/) for deadlines and forms.

## Financial assistance

Help is available to participating families. See [Scholarships & Financial Assistance](/programs/financial-assistance/), or contact Matt Derry at <matt@nseaswim.com>.
