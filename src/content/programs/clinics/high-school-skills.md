Skills clinics are only for swimmers who are **not** currently registered with a USA Swimming team.

- **Who:** high school swimmers
- **Experience:** previous swim experience required
- **When:** Saturday mornings, 8:30 – 9:30 am
- **Coaching ratio:** 1 coach to 5 swimmers
