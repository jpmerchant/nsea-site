The following equipment is required for all swimmers.

If your family is unable to purchase equipment due to financial reasons please contact us at <scholarship@nseaswim.com>

Our preferred suppliers are FINIS & SwimmersBest, bulk equipment orders will be placed 2 times a year \[August / April\].

A few of the more commonly replaced items are kept in stock.

[Equipment Order Form](https://form.jotform.com/jpmerchant/equipment-order)
