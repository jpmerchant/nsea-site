### Do we need to commit to 12 months?

Short answer : **No**

We operate on a three session / season basis.

- Short Course Season (September - March)
- Spring Season (April - May)\*
- Summer Season (June - July)\*

The Spring and Summer Seasons are **opt-out**. (See the calendar for opt-out deadlines)

\* Swimmers that don't opt-in to the Spring or Summer Seasons **can still participate**in swim meets during those seasons.

### Does my swimmer need to be a USA Swimming Member?

Yes, NSEA Swim is a USA Swimming member team and all Team members are required to be USA Swimming members

### Can Seasons / Sessions be mixed?

Short Answer : Yes.

Longer answer: These are the possible combinations

- Short Course + Spring + Summer
- Short Course + Spring
- Short Course + Summer
- Short Course

### Are swim meets required?

No, swim meets are not required, but meets bring purpose to practice and they help give swimmers something to point towards every few weeks.
